The Cold Lake Water Balance Model (CLWBM) models the surface elevation of Cold Lake in Alberta and Saskatchewan, Canada under future climate scenarios.
CLBM was developed by Kevin Shook and Karl Lindenschmidt from the University of Saskatchewan.

As input forcings CLWBM requires outputs from CRHM models of Primrose Lake basin, the interlake basin, and Cold Lake basin. It also requires
monthly time series of withdrawals from Cold Lake.

CLWBM estimates  
1. The surface elevation and outflows from Primrose Lake,  
2. Streamflows between Primrose Lake and Cold Lake, and
3. Surace elevations and outflows from Cold Lake

on a daily basin for the specified period.
